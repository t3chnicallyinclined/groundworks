# 2026-09-15: how the idea came together

Generic notes. The first instance was a private appellate matter; no matter content is recorded here.

## Starting point
- A private appeal needed every line of eight hearing transcripts, the court file, and tens of thousands of text messages labeled for how each bears on the order under review.
- Two earlier projects supplied parts. The **Forgily Book Scholar** read thousands of books with an RLM-style agent, a phase curriculum and quality gates. **SUPERGUN re_kb** is a reverse-engineering knowledge graph with ranked evidence, a datastore promotion gate, recorded dead ends and outcomes on edges.

## Key realizations, in order
1. **The two projects solve opposite problems.** The Book Scholar is a reading engine: it checks whether output has the right shape. re_kb is a belief engine: it checks whether a claim may be believed. The merge is agents that read everything and propose anything, with nothing becoming a conclusion unless a mechanical gate against the source passes.
2. **The Book Scholar's quality gates check shape, not truth.** The fix was a verbatim validator: every important label's quote must appear word for word in its unit. It caught real misquotes during the first run, and agents repaired them before the run passed.
3. **Coverage must be provable.** "Every unit exactly once" is checked by a program. This is the difference from retrieval.
4. **Evidence needs more than one axis.** Fidelity (exact words), status (usable at all: in the record, received, admissible) and authority (binding vs persuasive; verified in official text) are independent. In the instance, most of the important messages turned out to be unusable because they were never received in evidence. Neither side could use them.
5. **Adversarial roles belong inside the gate.** A conclusion is not ready until attacked, with no fatal head standing and every damaging head answered.
6. **Negative results need wording care.** A ruled-out claim's statement is the false claim. One agent read a ruled-out entry as asserted and raised a false alarm. The fix was to display these as "ELIMINATED CLAIM (false)".
7. **Hooks make the graph speak at the moment of error.** A tool call that names a citation receives the known labels, authority status and eliminated claims before it runs. In practice this surfaced unverified and misattributed authorities while documents were being written.
8. **Labels must be in the expert's language.** The primary lens is the domain's own authoritative framework, transcribed from its source (in the instance, the factors the court said it weighed). A crosswalk files every labeled item under it, producing a factor matrix split by usable and unusable.
9. **autoresearch adds the missing loop.** A goal file humans edit, one thing the agent edits, a fixed evaluator the agent cannot edit, keep or discard with a log, and simpler wins ties. For knowledge work the evaluator is a stack: mechanical gates as law, a fixed judged panel as advice, and a real outcome (such as a time split) where one exists.
10. **Lenses are projections, not versions.** Different experts see the same evidence through views and their own overlays. Their disagreement is data.
11. **It generalizes by answering eight questions:** goal, unit, fidelity ladder, status axis, vocabulary, gates, roles, evaluator.
12. **It is not RAG.** Retrieval is one way to use the graph. The method is exhaustive verified reading plus a truth-maintained, gated graph with adversarial roles and lenses.
13. **Cross-field discovery** (does paper X solve paper Y's problem?) is literature-based discovery. The gates, dead ends and a time-split evaluator address that field's chronic false-positive problem.
14. **Self-improvement belongs inside a fence.** Agents may improve guides and prompts. Humans own the gates, ladders, evaluator and privacy rules.

## Measured in instance 1
- Stage 1: 9,101 units; 77 agents; 77 of 77 chunks passed the validator on independent re-validation; about 7.4M subagent tokens; about 25 minutes wall clock.
- Load: 15,455 lines, 6,662 turns, 21,332 messages, 792 pages, 5,693 evidence links; 0 failed statements.
- Gate probe suites: all probes behaved (rejections and acceptances).
- Regression suite from past errors: 9 mechanical passes, 3 manual checks, 0 failures.
- Framework lens: 6,994 filings.

## Open questions carried forward
- **Label accuracy is unmeasured.** Double-labeling and adjudication come first.
- **The evaluator for domains without an outcome** is undecided.
- **Whether a cheap-model triage tier** preserves recall on important units is untested.
